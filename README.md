# PeekHealth
Shows the health of the mob or player you look at or attack — in your action bar. Paper 1.21+/26.x.
Look-mode (crosshair raytrace, wall-aware), damage-mode (melee & projectiles), or both. Hearts bar, MiniMessage & legacy formats, per-player toggle (persisted), entity blacklist, world blacklist. One tiny jar, no dependencies.
Commands: `/peekhealth` (toggle, players) · `/peekhealth reload`

## Tested on
Paper 1.21.11 and Paper 26.2 (runtime-tested, not just "it loads").
