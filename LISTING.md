**Titel:** PeekHealth — Action Bar Health Display [1.21–26.2]
**Tagline:** See any mob's or player's health in your action bar — look at it or hit it. Lightweight successor to the abandoned action-bar-health plugins.
**Kern:** The classic "Action Bar Health" plugin (677k downloads) hasn't been updated since 2023. PeekHealth is a modern rebuild for Paper 1.21+ and 26.x: crosshair + damage detection, hearts bar, MiniMessage formats, per-player toggle, zero dependencies. Actively maintained for every new Minecraft version.
